how it works, in order

A hackathon isn't one task. It's a dozen, spread over weeks.

So here they are, in the order they happen. At every step Caddie does the legwork and you make the call, and each step leaves something you can look back at.

The steps

01Find 02Read the rules 03Plan 04Build 05Check 06Submit

It reads the listings, so nothing open slips past you.

Hackathons that are open right now, narrowed to the ones you can actually enter with your skills and the hours you have. Every "good fit" comes with the reasons behind it.

Step 03PlanIn the app: Plan

One plan, in plain words. Nothing gets built until you say yes.

For a hackathon or just your own idea, Caddie writes what it will build, what it leaves out, and how long it should take. Your yes applies to that exact version, and if the plan tries to grow later, Caddie flags it.

Step 04BuildIn the app: Build

Caddie builds it on its own computer. You make the big calls.

The work is split between helpers that each do one job, on a separate, sealed computer, never on yours. You can have it stop after each feature to show you. Anything that publishes, deletes or changes your main code always waits for your yes.

Where it runs
Its own computer
The code it writes never runs on your machine.
"It works"
Tested, not claimed
Caddie saying it's done doesn't count. The app has to pass real checks in a real browser.
If it's stuck
Stops after 3 tries
Three failed fixes for the same problem, and it stops to ask you.

Step 05CheckIn the app: Check

Proof that it works, scored the way the judges will score it.

Each thing the judges look for is checked against the hackathon's own judging rules, with the proof attached. Score it yourself too, and Caddie shows where the two of you disagree.

Judged against
The real rules
The hackathon's own judging criteria, not a generic checklist.
Anything missing
Left blank
No made-up video links, no made-up licences, no "chance of winning".
Proof
On every check
A screenshot or test result you can open, not a promise.

Step 06SubmitIn the app: Submit

Then it stops, and you press Submit.

Your code goes to a separate branch in your own GitHub. It never overwrites your work, and adding it to your main code is your call. Your entry is laid out ready to paste into Devpost, with anything still missing clearly marked.

Your code
In your GitHub
A new branch for each change. Nothing else in the project is touched.

If changes clash, they come back for you to look at.

changes

privacy

what caddie will never do

The club stays in your hands.

  • ✕It won't swing for you. The build, the demo and the pitch are yours.
  • ✕It won't spend money, publish or change your main code without your yes, asked at the moment it matters.
  • ✕It won't call a rule "fine" if it couldn't read it. Anything it couldn't read is marked unknown.
