Logo & character · proposal

Yes — give Caddie a face, and make its face tell you what it’s doing.

Caddie works for you in the background: it reads rules, writes plans and builds apps. A character shows who is doing the work and what it’s doing right now without making you read a status line. That only works if the character stays small and every movement means something.

  • A soft blue block in Caddie’s own blue. It still reads clearly as a 16px browser icon.
  • A little golf flag on top. A caddie walks the course with you, and the flag marks the hole you’re aiming for. The flag is the part that moves most.
  • The eyes do the talking. Eight states, each tied to something Caddie is actually doing. It never acts cute for no reason.

Eight states, eight real meanings

Pick one to see it large. Each state appears only when Caddie is doing that thing: in the chat header, the mobile input and the Caddie tab.

How it’s built

One 64-unit drawing with no extra artwork per state. The eyes, mouth and flag swap or move, so the motion is cheap to build and stays consistent.

Body
Rounded block, 46 × 41.5 units, corner radius 18. Caddie blue #4d9fff fading to #2f7ddd.
Flag
Amber #f0a83c on a blue pole. It waves while building, droops when stuck and perks up when you type.
Eyes
Two dark rounded bars with a highlight. Each state swaps them for happy arcs, closed arcs or flat lines.
Extras
Thinking dots, a “needs you” badge and sleep z’s sit outside the body, so the outline never changes.
Small sizes
At 24px and below, the cheeks and shine drop out so the face stays crisp.
Motion
Loops for ongoing work last under a second. When idle it blinks about every 5 seconds. With Reduce Motion turned on, it holds still.

Sizes and app icon

Lockups

The wordmark is Inter Bold with tight letter spacing, matching the product UI. The character always sits to the left of the word or above it, never inside it.

Where it shows up

Do
  • Next to Caddie’s name in the chat, and on its messages
  • The Caddie tab and the always-on input on mobile
  • The welcome screen and empty screens (“nothing here yet”)
  • App icon, browser icon and sign-in

Where it doesn’t

Don’t
  • Beside headings or on every card — one per area at most
  • As the icon for a feature (Brief, Discover and the rest keep plain icons)
  • In a new colour, outfit or pose drawn for one screen
  • Animating when nothing is happening, beyond the idle blink
