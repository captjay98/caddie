My recommendation

Keep the flag block as the logo

It is the only one that stays clear at 16px. It also says “caddie” without looking like a sports app, and the flag gives a natural reason for every movement: aiming, waving while it builds, drooping when it’s stuck.

The talking bubble is the strongest idea for a chat-first product, but it looks too much like the chat and message icons. Next to the speech-bubble icons in the app, people would read it as “messages”, not “Caddie”.

Use the ball in a cap for moments, not as the logo

The cap is the most charming, and it’s good for one-off big moments: the welcome screen, “You submitted!” and empty screens. It gets crowded below 24px and adds a second colour mass, so it’s weaker as an icon.

If you pick it anyway, it still works in the app because the faces and states are shared. Your choice above is saved and used by the welcome introduction, so you can see it in place.
