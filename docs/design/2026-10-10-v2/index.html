Caddie v2 · for review · 9 Oct 2026 · not in code

Chat comes first, the brief is short, and the words are plain

This version goes back to the Today-style layout. Caddie is the first tab and is always one tap away. The brief replaces Home. Every screen shows less, with nothing important removed. v1 is still in caddie-redesign/ so you can compare.

What changed from v1

1 · DesktopPages on the left, Caddie always on the right

Brief, Discover and Projects are tabs across the top. The Caddie chat stays open on the right of every page and remembers what you were talking about. It takes 40% of the window by default; drag its left edge to resize it (340px up to 60%, and the page always keeps 520px to read). Double-click the edge, or use Settings › Appearance, to reset. You can also shrink it to a small character, or open it full screen.

2 · MobileCaddie · Brief · Discover · Projects, with an input above the tabs

Caddie is the first and main tab. Every other tab has a “Ask Caddie…” bar just above the tab bar. Tapping it opens the chat about whatever you’re looking at. This replaces v1’s Home-first order.

3 · Brief replaces HomeA short morning paper, not a dashboard

Shows the date and a title, then up to three cards. Each card has a coloured label, one headline, two or three sentences with the key facts in bold, and one button. Below that: deadlines as coloured rows, and finished items folded away.

4 · Less on every screenDetails fold away

Prizes, sources, older plan versions, build logs and code all sit behind “more” sections or a “Code — for developers” tab. Each screen leads with one decision, and secondary content is there when you ask for it.

5 · Beginner-friendly wordsPlan · Build · Check · Submit

“Prove” becomes Check, “Ship” becomes Submit (or Share for personal projects), revisions become versions, runs become builds. The full word list is below. The same terms appear everywhere, including in Caddie’s replies.

6 · A face for CaddieA small character whose face shows what it’s doing

A soft blue block carrying a little golf flag, with eight states (ready, listening, thinking, building, needs you, done, stuck, paused). It shows up only where Caddie itself appears: the chat, the input bar, the tab, sign-in and empty screens.

Plan

Still open

  • Backend still needed (carried over from v1): an account-wide brief, a spending endpoint, saving hackathons without entering them, plan content with stored decisions, a preview link, and a to-do API. v2 adds two: one chat per “Talking about”, and the in-chat “OK to spend?” question.
  • Sample content: the hackathons are real Devpost listings. The projects, builds, costs and the app shown in previews are made up. Ledger Nudge is the only fully filled-in project. Founder Hours and Pantry Scale change the headers, next steps, Check and Submit mode, but reuse Ledger’s preview and Submit fields.
  • New in this round, also needs backend: searching inside chats (there’s no message search today, so the results page says so); Caddie scoring the project itself against the rubric (the app has no automated judge run yet; only your own scores are designed); a “tell me when this tool is ready” signal for Build tools.
  • The character is a proposal. Before handing it to the app, it still needs a hand-tuned 16px browser icon and an app-icon version with a safe area.
