## What the person wants (their own words)

Our neighbourhood mutual-aid group shares spare food so it doesn't go to waste.
I want a shared board for our group: a member posts "2 bags of rice, collect by
Friday", and other members of the group can see it and mark that they've claimed
it. We're a group, so everyone in our group sees the same board and can post,
but a different neighbourhood's group shouldn't see our listings. One of us
should be the group's organiser who can remove a posting that's gone off.

## Complete when

- I can sign in with an emailed code.
- I can create a group (our neighbourhood) as its organiser and add members.
- Any member can post a food listing (item, quantity, collect-by) and it saves.
- After a restart, every listing is still there with the same values.
- A member can mark a listing claimed; the organiser can delete any listing.
- A member can delete their own listing; a plain member cannot delete someone
  else's (only the organiser can).
- Another group cannot see, claim, or delete our group's listings.
- Every screen shows what the server saved.
