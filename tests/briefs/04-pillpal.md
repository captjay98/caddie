## What the person wants (their own words)

My dad takes several pills and keeps forgetting which ones he's taken. I want a
very simple app where he can list his medicines — the name, the dose, and what
times of day he takes them — and then each day tick off the ones he's taken so
far. Big clear text, nothing fiddly. It has to be his own list that stays saved;
if the app reloads it can't just forget everything like the last one we tried.

## Complete when

- He can sign in with an emailed code and see only his own medicines.
- He can add a medicine (name, dose, times of day) and it saves.
- After the app reloads, his medicines and today's ticks are still there.
- A medicine can be edited and deleted.
- He can mark a medicine as taken for today and untick it.
- Another signed-in person cannot see, change, or delete his medicines.
- Every screen shows what the server saved, with large readable text.
