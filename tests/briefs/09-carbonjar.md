# CarbonJar

- id: carbonjar
- size: small
- source: https://climatechangemakers2026.devpost.com
- theme: Climate action (The Climate Change-Makers Challenge 2026 — "create solutions for our climate crisis")
- paired: no
- teamMode: off

## What the person wants (their own words)

I want to understand my own carbon footprint day to day. A simple app where I
log an activity — a car trip, a flight, a meat meal, electricity used — pick its
type and a rough amount, and the app records a rough CO2 estimate for it. Then a
weekly total so I can see whether I'm improving. It's my own log, private to me.

## Complete when

- I can sign in with an emailed code and only see my own entries.
- I can log an activity (type, amount) and it saves with its CO2 estimate.
- After a restart, my entries are still there with the same values.
- An entry can be edited and deleted.
- Another signed-in person cannot see, change, or delete my entries.
- There is a weekly-total view computed from saved entries.
- Every screen shows what the server saved.
