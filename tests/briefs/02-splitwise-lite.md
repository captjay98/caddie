## What the person wants (their own words)

When my friends and I travel we always lose track of who paid for what. I want
an app for a trip where everyone in the group adds the things they paid for —
"I paid 60 for dinner", "she paid 20 for the taxi" — and the app works out who
owes whom at the end so we settle up fairly. We're a group, so everyone on the
trip should see all the trip's expenses, but a person who isn't on our trip
must never see our numbers. I'd also love one line at the top that explains in
plain words where the money went this trip.

## Complete when

- I can sign in with an emailed code.
- I can create a trip (the group) and I'm its owner; I can add the people on it.
- Anyone on the trip can add an expense (who paid, amount, what for) and it saves.
- After a restart, every trip expense is still there with the same values.
- An expense can be edited and deleted by the trip's members.
- A person who is not on the trip cannot see, add to, change, or delete the
  trip's expenses, and the trip never shows up in their list.
- The app shows a settle-up summary (who owes whom) computed from saved data.
- There is one plain-language line summarising the trip's spending.
- Every screen shows what the server saved.
