## What the person wants (their own words)

I'm revising for exams and I want my own flashcard app. I make decks — like
"Biology chapter 3" — and inside each deck I add cards with a question on the
front and the answer on the back. Then I can study a deck: it shows me the
question, I flip it, and I mark whether I got it right. I want it to remember my
cards and which ones I keep getting wrong. My decks are mine; classmates signing
in shouldn't see my decks. It'd be nice if the app could suggest a card or two
for a topic I type in, to get me started.

## Complete when

- I can sign in with an emailed code and only see my own decks.
- I can create a deck and add cards (question, answer) to it; both save.
- After a restart, my decks and cards are still there with the same values.
- A card can be edited and deleted; a deck can be deleted.
- A study session lets me flip a card and mark right/wrong, and that is saved.
- Another signed-in person cannot see, change, or delete my decks or cards.
- The AI "suggest cards for a topic" feature returns cards I can save.
- Every screen shows what the server saved.
