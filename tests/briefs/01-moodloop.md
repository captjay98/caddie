- paired: no

## What the person wants (their own words)

I want a little app where I can check in on my mood a few times a day. I tap
how I'm feeling — happy, okay, low, anxious — add a short note about why, and
it saves it. Then I want to see my week on a simple chart so I can tell if
things are getting better or worse. It's private; nobody else should ever see
my check-ins. Let me sign in with my email so my check-ins follow me on my
phone and my laptop.

## Complete when

- I can sign in with an emailed code and only see my own check-ins.
- I can add a mood check-in (feeling + a short note) and it saves.
- After I close and reopen the app, my past check-ins are still there.
- I can edit a check-in's note and delete a check-in.
- Another signed-in person cannot see, change, or delete my check-ins.
- There is a week view that shows my check-ins over the last 7 days.
- Every screen shows the check-ins the server actually saved, not a local copy.
